# Anatomy atlas artwork and reuse

These are **educational illustrations**, not medical scans, diagnoses, or data extracted from the camera user.

- `body_atlas.png` was rasterized from **SVG Human With All Organs.svg** (Wikimedia Commons), uploaded by Madhero88 and assembled from work credited to LadyofHats, ZooFari, Piom, Patrick.lynch, and Mikael Häggström. The full head-to-toe art was enlarged and exported with a transparent background for offline use. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SVG_Human_With_All_Organs.svg . License: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported** (CC BY-SA 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . This derived PNG is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license. The app recolors and glows it at display time; no author endorsement is implied.
- `brain_atlas.png` was rasterized from **Brain Lateral (NIH BioArt 60).svg** by **Ryan Kissinger / NIAID BioArt**. It was enlarged and exported with transparency. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brain_Lateral_(NIH_BioArt_60).svg ; original: https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/60 . The source is listed as **US public domain** (US federal government work).

Labels, highlights, interactions, and any visible holographic glow are software overlays. The organ illustration is stylized and is not a clinically accurate map of any particular person's body.
